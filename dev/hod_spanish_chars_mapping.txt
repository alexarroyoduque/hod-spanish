Harmony of Dissonance (USA) - Spanish font v9
Base ROM CRC32: 88C1B562

v9 principle:
- Keeps the simplified v8 menu support.
- Adds lowercase ñ to the small/menu font.
- Menu support is now limited to: á é í ó ú Ó ñ.
- Á É Í Ú ü Ñ ¿ ¡ remain dialogue-only.
- Small-font blank/fallback index 0x3A remains untouched.
- Only one menu symbol is sacrificed for ñ: the star symbol at code 0x819A.

Menu + dialogue mapping:
Ó = 0x827A | small idx 0x1A
á = 0x829C | small idx 0x3B
é = 0x829D | small idx 0x3C
í = 0x829E | small idx 0x3D
ó = 0x829F | small idx 0x3E
ú = 0x82A0 | small idx 0x3F
ñ = 0x819A | reuses the star-symbol slot in large and small fonts

Dialogue-only mapping:
Á = 0x82A4
É = 0x82A5
Í = 0x82A6
Ú = 0x82A8
Ñ = 0x82A9
¡ = 0x82AE
¿ = 0x82B0
ü = 0x82B8

Renderer patches inherited from v8:
ROM 0x143DC: CMP #0x19 -> CMP #0x1A
ROM 0x14404: CMP #0x19 -> CMP #0x1F

Important:
- The original star symbol mapped by 0x819A is no longer available in the menu/dialogue font; it is used for ñ.
- ¿ and ¡ remain dialogue-only by design.
- Apply directly to the clean USA ROM; do not stack v3-v8 first.
- Use text_hod_spanish_v9.rb as DSVEdit/dsvlib/text.rb (rename to text.rb).
