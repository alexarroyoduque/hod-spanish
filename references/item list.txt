ITEM LIST
==============================================================================

This document presents you a complete list of all the items you can find in 
the game, arranged by type. These include equipment, expendable items, 
rarities, relics, and collectibles for decorating Juste's Room in the Castle 
Treasury.

Items are listed as they are in the game menu. The order in which defensive 
items are arranged goes like this: first come all the items that increase 
Defense only, listed in order of increasing protection, then come those that 
increase Defense as well as other stats. That's why you'll find some less 
defensive items listed after the most powerful armors. 

Note that I've not described each item in detail. I did this for two simple 
reasons: first, the in-game explanation tells about all there is to say, and 
second, each effect can be easily verified by the player.

For every item, you'll find info about where it can be found or bought, or 
what monsters may drop it. This doesn't apply to healing items, which can be 
found in many places, or bought from the Merchant.
