Hopefully this Inventory Translation Guide will assist those of you that
cannot wait until September '02 to play this fine gem of a game. It truly
incorporates some of the best elements of both Symphony of the Night and
Circle of the Moon. Pity about its music and its use of bright colours :/

Note that this game is also know as White Night Concerto (an overly literal
translation) and its English name, Harmony of Dissonance.
