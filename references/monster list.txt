MONSTER ENCYCLOPEDIA
==============================================================================

The following is a complete list of the 100 different enemies you can 
encounter throughout the game. They are listed as they are in the in-game 
Monster Encyclopedia, and the info is also the same, apart from the indication 
of the area(s) where you can find each monster. To visualize these info in the 
game, you must first collect the Monster Tome relic in The Wailing Way A , 
then access the menu and select "Secret Info" and "Encyclopedia". 

"A" and "B" indicate each of the two castles in the game.

A "---" in the "Drop items" list means "nothing".

Concerning experience, you may notice that from a certain moment onwards, 
enemies will give you less EXP than that that is listed. The reason behind it 
is this: an enemy will give you the listed amount of EXP points if your 
experience level is lesser than, or equal to, his own. When your level gets 
higher than that of a monster, that monster will give you less than the listed 
EXP when killed. According to Zach Keene's FAQ, any monster will give you only 
1 EXP point when killed since the moment you have roughly 10 levels more than 
him.
